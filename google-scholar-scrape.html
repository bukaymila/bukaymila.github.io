Scholar Scraper

Imagined by Alim Yakub (RP) for the Cold Box Project
📖 How This Works

Paste a Google Scholar profile URL (it looks like https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=XXXXXXXX) and click Scrape & Download.

The backend fetches every publication sorted by date, parses the title, authors, journal, and year from each row, and returns them as a plain-text file.

The downloaded .txt is formatted as one block per work, separated by a blank line:

Paper Title
Author One, Author Two
Journal Name
2024

Next Paper Title
...

⚠️ The backend runs on a free tier and sleeps after 15 minutes of inactivity. The first request after a break can take up to a minute while it wakes up.
